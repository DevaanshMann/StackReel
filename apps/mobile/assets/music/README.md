# Daybreak

Daybreak is an original 34-second instrumental at 120 BPM. The electric keys, plucked melody, bass, and percussion were synthesized for Stack Reel.

The composition and recording are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Creators can use, edit, and publish the music in their reels without attribution.
